# Character Lock — 雾林星球 / Mist Orbit

- **Form:** one small floating round guide, a slightly irregular orb with a soft inner crescent glow; no face, no limbs, no text.
- **Silhouette:** circular at small sizes, with a single off-center light bloom; never becomes a planet with rings or a human-like character.
- **Palette:** forest deep `#173C36`, mist `#DCEAE4`, moon-lilac `#AFA7EA`, quiet mint `#8DD9AF`, warm dawn `#F1B79A` used sparingly.
- **Materials:** frosted translucent glass with internal haze; diffuse rather than glossy; gentle bloom only.
- **Default behavior:** a quiet, slow pulse; shown as a small header/status accent or a restrained hero guide.
- **Forbidden drift:** faces, eyes, limbs, animal features, logos, wordmarks, hard neon, chrome, gradients that overpower text, or a central mascot larger than primary content.
