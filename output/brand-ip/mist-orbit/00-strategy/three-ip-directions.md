# Three Brand-IP Directions

| Direction | Form & promise | Material language | Score | Decision |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 雾林星球 / Mist Orbit | A quiet luminous guide that helps users find a breathable rhythm. Small round silhouette, emotionally neutral, easy to scale into states. | Frosted glass, mist, moonlit green, pale lilac. | 31/35 | **Selected** |
| 拾光纸鹤 / Lightfold Crane | A folded paper companion that marks small moments of recovery. More crafted and editorial. | Warm paper, cream, ink-blue, soft gold. | 27/35 | Not selected: less suited to immersive night soundscapes. |
| 潮汐种子 / Tidal Seed | A soft seed that changes with breath and tidal cycles. More playful and tactile. | Matte rubber, watery teal, coral highlights. | 26/35 | Not selected: more playful than the supplied references’ quiet tone. |

Selection basis: Mist Orbit best supports small-size recognition, dark and light UI extensibility, restrained motion, and the supplied nature-led references. Name availability and trademark clearance have not been checked.
